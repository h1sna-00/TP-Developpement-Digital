Will search engines index the hodden answers?

Yes, search engines will fully index the hidden answers, but with one crucial condition: the text must be present in the initial HTML document when the page first loads.

Can I open more than one question at a time?

Yes, you can open more than one question at a time, depending on whether you group them or leave them independent.

Does it work JavaScript disabled?

Yes, it works perfectly with JavaScript completely disabled. Because the (details) and (summary) elements are native to the browser's HTML engine, all of the interactive behaviors—including clicking to expand, keyboard toggling, and closing other panels in a group—are handled directly by the browser.
